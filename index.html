About Vila Kartal

Experience the perfect blend of traditional Dalmatian charm and modern comfort on the Adriatic coast.

Nestled in the charming coastal town of Tribunj, Vila Kartal offers a perfect blend of traditional Dalmatian architecture and modern comfort. Our villa provides an ideal base for exploring the pristine beaches, crystal-clear waters, and rich cultural heritage of the Croatian coast.

Whether you're seeking a romantic getaway, a family vacation, or a peaceful retreat, our dedicated team ensures every detail is taken care of with warmth and personal attention.
