Local Guide

Discover Tribunj & Surroundings

Welcome to Tribunj! Here are our top recommendations for activities and attractions you shouldn't miss during your stay.

In Tribunj (Walking Distance)

Old Village on the Island

The historic core is situated on a small island connected by an old stone bridge. Perfect for a sunset stroll and dinner in an authentic tavern.

St. Nicholas Hill & Church

A short, steep walk to this 15th-century church rewards you with one of the most beautiful panoramic views of the Adriatic coast.

Zamalin & Bristak Beaches

Enjoy pristine waters! Zamalin is a local favorite surrounded by pine trees, while Bristak is perfect for families with children.

Traditional Donkey Race

If you visit in early August, don't miss this hilarious and unique local tradition that draws crowds every year.

Nearby Excursions

Krka National Park

An absolute must-see! Just a 20-minute drive away, offering spectacular waterfalls (Skradinski Buk) and boat rides to Visovac island.

City of Šibenik (UNESCO)

Visit this medieval city to explore the famous St. James Cathedral and its magnificent defensive fortresses offering breathtaking views.

City of Vodice

Our neighboring town, accessible via a lovely seaside promenade. Known for its vibrant nightlife, large marina, and excellent restaurants.

Boat Excursions & Islands

Kornati National Park

A nautical paradise consisting of 89 islands. Full-day boat tours depart daily, including cruising, swimming in crystal-clear waters, and lunch on board.

Prvić Island

A peaceful, car-free island just a short boat ride away. Visit the Memorial Center dedicated to Faust Vrančić, the famous Croatian inventor.

Nature & Active Holidays

Vrana Lake Nature Park

Croatia's largest natural lake. A fantastic spot for cycling enthusiasts, fishing, and bird watching. The Kamenjak viewpoint offers unforgettable vistas.

Dubrava Falconry Center

Located in a dense pine forest near Šibenik, this center offers a unique, educational, and up-close experience with majestic birds of prey.
